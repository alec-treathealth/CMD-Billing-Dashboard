# BH / SUD Payer Policy Report — All National Payers

**Window:** 2026-07-30 → 2026-08-09
**Scope:** UnitedHealthcare/Optum, Aetna, Cigna/Evernorth, BCBS/Anthem/Carelon.
**Not researched:** Lucet, Magellan, navigation TPAs (Quantum Health / Accolade / Included Health), Transparency in Coverage / MRF.
**Compiled:** 2026-08-09 · KB last updated: 2026-08-09

---

## TL;DR

- **No payer created, deleted, or reclassified a BH CPT or revenue code.** CPT is AMA-annual, revenue codes are NUBC, HCPCS/G-codes are CMS. Every item below is a change to *how existing codes are adjudicated*.
- **Highest-impact, hard date:** UHC will require a CPT/HCPCS on outpatient UB-04 claims for **93 NUBC-silent revenue codes**, effective **2026-10-01**, or the claim is ineligible for reimbursement. **090x BH codes are not on the list**; 0300, 0760/0762/0769, 0940 and the 027x supply block are.
- **Top OON item to chase:** Anthem's **"Use of a Nonparticipating Care Provider"** commercial facility administrative policy, effective **2026-07-01**. Surfaced from the New York feed; state-by-state coverage unknown.
- **Structural discovery:** Anthem publishes provider news **per state**, not nationally. Any tracker that checks "Anthem" as one source is systematically missing changes.
- **Payer-mix event:** Cigna discontinues medical Individual & Family Plans effective **2027-01-01** — cross-posted to the Evernorth BH channel, so BH providers are the intended audience.

## Recommendations

1. **Run the UHC 93-code exposure query before 2026-10-01.** This is the only item with a hard date, a hard consequence, and a fully enumerated code list. Everything else is watch-and-wait.
2. **Pull the Anthem "Use of a Nonparticipating Care Provider" policy for every state you bill.** It is the only OON-specific *policy* found across all four payers this window, and you currently have it from one state's feed.
3. **Re-point Anthem monitoring at state feeds.** Tracking "Anthem" as one source guarantees blind spots.
4. **Confirm Aetna's BH precert list status.** A 2024-dated list on a live payer page is either an error or a consolidation. Both matter.
5. **Model the Cigna IFP exit if marketplace volume is non-trivial.** 2027-01-01 is far enough out to plan for and close enough to budget.
6. **Do not act on:** the Routine Test Management policies, the ePA standardization commitment, or the Carelon fee-reduction figures.

**Benchmarks that would change this:** any payer adding a 090x code to a required-coding list; a NUBC 090x revision; an Aetna September OfficeLink with BH content; publication of Anthem's nonparticipating-provider policy in additional states.

## Caveats

- **10-day window.** Thin diffs are expected; absence of change here is weak evidence about longer trends.
- **Verified vs. carried forward:** every item marked "Added 2026-08-09" was read from a primary source during this run. Items carried from the 2026-07-30 seed were not re-verified.
- **Pre-window items included:** several Anthem and Cigna items predate the window and are flagged as such. They are included because they were absent from the knowledge base, not because they are new.
- **Secondary sources not promoted to fact:** Carelon fee-reduction percentages and Carelon claim-routing mechanics come from third-party provider guides.
- **Login-gated:** CignaforHCP.com, Availity, and portions of Provider Express were not accessible. Absence of a published change is not proof none exists.
- **Not researched at all:** Lucet, Magellan, navigation TPAs, and the entire Transparency-in-Coverage / MRF dimension — still the largest gap in this knowledge base.

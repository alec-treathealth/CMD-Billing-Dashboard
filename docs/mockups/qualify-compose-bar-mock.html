What changed and why. The bar is one console instead of four floating pickers. The vertical seam is the whole idea: Payer and Facility sit in a tinted zone because they move the scores; Employer and Funding sit on plain white because they only narrow the list. That's Design B made visible, so the hint line Phase 2 owed becomes unnecessary. The dark readout bar anchors the console and gives the match count somewhere to live with real weight, alongside the evidence gauge — four pips that fill as client count climbs. One colour throughout: evidence is signalled by how many pips are solid versus hollow, never by hue, so it survives greyscale, colour-blindness, and the dark bar. Patient count is what the last two sessions proved actually governs whether a number is honest, so it gets a physical form rather than a footnote. The PHI row is hatched and lock-badged so identified search always looks different from aggregate search. A dashed chip with ↳ marks a filter added by a ticker click rather than typed.
